# Aleoris Technologies

**Building software for markets, data, and automation.**

Aleoris Technologies is an independent technology company focused on building **high-performance software, data-driven systems, and intelligent automation**.

We explore the intersection of:

* 📊 **Financial technology** — market data, trading systems, and quantitative tools
* ⚡ **High-performance systems** — real-time data processing, low-latency architectures, and distributed workloads
* 🤖 **Automation & AI** — turning complex processes into reliable software
* 🔬 **Research & experiments** — testing ideas, technologies, and unconventional approaches

We believe in **building, measuring, and iterating** — turning ideas into working systems rather than leaving them on the drawing board.

### 🛠 What we build

Our repositories contain experiments, libraries, infrastructure, and production-oriented projects developed along the way.

Some projects are open source. Others are research or building blocks for larger systems.
